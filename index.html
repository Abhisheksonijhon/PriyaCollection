Our Story

Priya
Collection

Priya Collection is a family-run fashion boutique based in Dhar, Madhya Pradesh — just a short drive from Indore. Founded with a simple belief: every woman deserves to feel beautiful, whether she's dressing for a Wednesday market run or a Saturday wedding reception.

Owner Jalaj Jain built Priya Collection on values of quality, honest pricing and genuine personal service. Every piece in the store is handpicked — from crisp cotton kurtis and ruffle tops for daily wear, to embroidered silk suit sets and elegant evening gowns for life's bigger moments.

We also serve wholesale and gifting — coordinated return-gift sets for weddings, bulk orders for festive seasons, and shopkeeper-friendly pricing for resellers across MP.
